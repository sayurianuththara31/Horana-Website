LOCATION

Horana is situated in the Kalutara District within the Western Province of Sri Lanka. Positioned approximately 42 kilometers southeast of Colombo, Horana serves as a key junction connecting major commercial routes.

Its strategic geographic placement makes it a vital transport and trade gateway between coastal commercial hubs and interior agricultural regions.

Key Geographic Details

  • District Kalutara
  • Province Western Province
  • Distance from Colombo ~42 km
  • Elevation 28 m above sea level
