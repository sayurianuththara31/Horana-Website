Prominent Natural Feature: Wanahamige Galguhawa

A Hidden Ancient Heritage in the Dense Forest: Wanahamige Galguhawa, Horana

An extraordinary creation of nature, this ancient cave was once a tranquil sanctuary for meditating yogis and remains a guardian of mysterious traces of prehistoric human existence. Wanahamige Galguhawa in Horana is a magnificent historical landmark—a destination open to true nature explorers seeking the wonders of the wilderness and history enthusiast tourists treading the pathways of the past.
