HISTORY

Horana is a Historic city in the Kalutara District with a rich heritage connected to the ancient Raigam Kingdom. It has evolved from a royal agricultural hub into a prominent modern administrative and Industrial center in the western province.

Historical Monument

Origin of the name 'Horana'

  • Abundance of Hora and Na Trees: In ancient times, the region was densely populated with Hora and Na trees, leading to "Hora-Na," which evolved into "Horana".
  • The Settlement of Prince Veranaga: Another tradition holds that the area derived its name from being the dwelling place of Prince Veranaga (Veranaga → Horana).
  • The Sounding of the Horn (Horanae): It is said that horns (horanae) were blown here to assemble Prince Veranaga's army, giving rise to the name.
  • Transformation of Girassandamalaya: Known during King Vijayabahu I's era as "Girassandamalaya," it later became "Swarnamalaya" and eventually transformed into "Horana".
  • The Forest of Thieves (Hora-Arana): According to legend, the dense forest once served as a hideout for bandits, thus known as "Hora-Arana" (Forest of Thieves).
